----------------------------------------------------
  Race Condition vs synchronized and AtomicInteger
----------------------------------------------------
4 threads each add Rs. 1 to a shared total 100000 times. Expected total = 400000
+----------------------------------+-------------+----------------------------+
| Approach                         | Final Total | Result                     |
+----------------------------------+-------------+----------------------------+
| No synchronization (race cond... | 385697      | WRONG (lost 14303 updates) |
| synchronized method              | 400000      | Correct                    |
| synchronized block               | 400000      | Correct                    |
| AtomicInteger (atomic operation) | 400000      | Correct                    |
+----------------------------------+-------------+----------------------------+
Total records: 4
The unsafe total is usually LOWER than expected because two threads read the same
old value and both write back old+1, so one update is lost. The bank's real
deposit/withdraw/transfer methods are synchronized to prevent exactly this.
